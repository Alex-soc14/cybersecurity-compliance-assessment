## ISO/IEC 27001 — Information Security Risk Management

### Assessment

The organization has established a risk register and uses a defined methodology to identify and assess information security risks.

However, the risk management process is not formally governed.

The risk assessment methodology has not been formally approved, risk acceptance criteria are not clearly defined, and responsibilities for individual risks are not consistently assigned.

The frequency for reviewing information security risks is also not formally established, and risk treatment decisions are not always documented.

**Status:** Non-Compliant

### Identified Gaps

1. The information security risk assessment methodology is not formally approved.
2. Formal risk acceptance criteria have not been defined.
3. Risk owners are not consistently assigned.
4. The frequency and conditions for reviewing information security risks are not formally established.
5. Risk treatment and acceptance decisions are not consistently documented.

### Recommended Actions

1. Formally approve and document the information security risk assessment methodology.
2. Define risk acceptance criteria and approval responsibilities.
3. Assign a risk owner to each identified risk.
4. Establish periodic and event-driven risk reviews.
5. Document risk treatment, acceptance and residual risk decisions.

### Evidence

- Information Security Risk Management Procedure
- Approved risk assessment methodology
- Risk register with assigned risk owners
- Risk acceptance criteria
- Risk treatment records
- Periodic risk review records
