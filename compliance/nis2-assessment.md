# NIS2 Assessment

## Assessment Methodology

The assessment evaluates selected NIS2 requirements against the
organization's current policies, processes and available evidence.

Each requirement is classified as:

- **Compliant** — requirement adequately addressed
- **Partially Compliant** — requirement partially addressed, with identified gaps
- **Non-Compliant** — requirement not adequately addressed

---

## NIS2 Article 21(2)(a)

### Requirement

Policies on risk analysis and information system security.

### Assessment

**Status:** Partially Compliant

### Evidence

- Risk assessment methodology exists
- Risk register is maintained
- No formally approved Information Security Policy
- Risk reviews are performed only occasionally

### Identified Gaps

1. Information Security Policy has not been formally approved.
2. Risk review frequency has not been formally defined.

### Risk

The absence of a formally approved security policy and structured
risk review process may result in inconsistent security practices
and insufficient monitoring of changes in the organization's risk
profile.

### Recommended Actions

- Develop and formally approve an Information Security Policy.
- Define roles and responsibilities for information security.
- Establish a defined periodic risk review process.
- Document and retain evidence of risk reviews and management approval.
