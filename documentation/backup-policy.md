# Backup Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that important company information can be recovered in case of data loss, system failure, cyberattack or other incidents.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to critical company data, information systems, applications and services that require backup and recovery.

## 3. Backup Requirements

Critical data shall be backed up regularly according to its importance and business requirements.

Backups shall be stored separately from the primary systems to reduce the risk of losing both the original data and the backups during an incident.

Access to backups shall be restricted to authorized personnel.

## 4. Backup Testing

Backup restoration shall be tested periodically to verify that data can be successfully recovered.

The results of restoration tests shall be documented, including any problems identified and corrective actions taken.

## 5. Disaster Recovery

The organization shall maintain a Disaster Recovery Procedure for restoring critical systems following a major incident.

Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO) shall be defined for critical systems.

Responsibilities for backup management and recovery activities shall be clearly assigned.

## 6. Policy Review

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually and following significant changes to systems, backup requirements or the organization's risk environment.
