# Access Control Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that access to company systems and information is granted only to authorized users and is appropriate for their role.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, company devices, information systems, applications and data managed by the organization.

## 3. Access Principles

Access rights shall be assigned according to the user's job responsibilities and the principle of least privilege.

Each user shall have an individual account and credentials must not be shared.

Administrative privileges shall only be granted when necessary and shall be limited to the required activities.

## 4. Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) shall be used for company accounts where technically and operationally appropriate.

MFA shall be mandatory for privileged accounts and should be progressively extended to all company accounts.

## 5. Access Reviews

User access rights shall be reviewed periodically to verify that permissions are still appropriate.

Excessive or unnecessary privileges shall be removed.

Access reviews and related changes shall be documented.

## 6. Joiner, Mover and Leaver Process

Access rights shall be created when a user joins the organization, updated when their role changes and revoked when their employment or relationship with the organization ends.

Accounts belonging to employees who leave the organization shall be disabled in a timely manner.

## 7. Policy Review

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually and following significant changes to the organization's systems, roles or security requirements.
