# Information Security Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish the principles and requirements necessary to protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of the organization's information and information systems.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees and contractors, as well as all company-owned devices, information systems, applications and information used or managed by the organization, including remote working environments.

## 3. Security Objectives

The organization aims to:

* Protect the confidentiality of information from unauthorized access or disclosure.
* Maintain the integrity of information and prevent unauthorized modification.
* Ensure the availability of information systems and services when required.
* Identify, assess and manage information security risks.
* Comply with applicable legal, regulatory and contractual requirements.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

### Management

Management is responsible for approving the Information Security Policy, providing appropriate resources and ensuring that information security objectives are aligned with the organization's business objectives.

### IT Department

The IT Department is responsible for implementing and maintaining technical security controls, managing information systems and access rights, and supporting the detection and response to security incidents.

### Employees

All employees are responsible for complying with information security policies and procedures, protecting their credentials and reporting suspected security incidents.

### Privacy Function

The Privacy function is responsible for supporting the organization in matters related to the protection of personal data and privacy requirements.

## 5. Risk Management

The organization shall maintain a formal Information Security Risk Management Procedure to identify, assess, treat and monitor information security risks.

Risk assessments shall be performed periodically and whenever significant changes occur to systems, processes or the threat environment.

Risk treatment decisions and residual risks shall be documented and assigned to appropriate risk owners.

## 6. Access Control

Access to company information systems shall be granted according to business requirements and the principle of least privilege.

Users shall have individual accounts and shall not share credentials.

Multi-factor authentication shall be implemented for company accounts where technically and operationally appropriate.

Access rights shall be periodically reviewed to ensure that permissions remain appropriate for the user's role.

Access rights shall be modified or revoked when employees change roles or leave the organization.

## 7. Incident Management

The organization shall maintain a formal Incident Management Procedure for the identification, reporting, assessment, response and resolution of information security incidents.

Security incidents shall be recorded in a centralized incident register, including relevant information about the incident, actions taken and resolution.

Roles and responsibilities for incident detection, response, escalation and communication shall be clearly defined.

Following significant incidents, the organization shall perform a post-incident review and document lessons learned and corrective actions.

## 8. Data Protection

The organization shall process personal data only for legitimate and defined purposes and in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

Personal data shall be collected only when necessary for the relevant processing purpose and shall be handled securely throughout its lifecycle.

Access to personal data shall be restricted to authorized personnel based on business requirements and the principle of least privilege.

Personal data shall be protected against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss and destruction.

The organization shall maintain appropriate procedures for managing personal data breaches and data subject requests.

## 9. Security Awareness and Training

The organization shall provide security awareness and cybersecurity training to employees during onboarding and on a periodic basis.

Training shall cover relevant information security risks, security policies, secure use of company systems and procedures for reporting security incidents.

Additional role-specific training shall be provided where required by job responsibilities or access to critical systems.

Training activities and completion records shall be maintained and the effectiveness of the awareness program shall be periodically reviewed.

## 10. Policy Review

The Information Security Policy shall be reviewed at least annually to ensure that it remains appropriate and effective.

The policy shall also be reviewed following significant changes to the organization, information systems, security risks, applicable legal or regulatory requirements, or following major security incidents.

Changes to the policy shall be formally approved and communicated to relevant personnel.
