# Processing Activities

## Records of Processing Activities (ROPA)

This document provides a simplified overview of the main personal data processing activities performed by FinTech Solutions S.r.l.

| Processing Activity                  | Data Subjects           | Personal Data                                                   | Purpose                                        | Recipients                                      | Retention                                          |
| ------------------------------------ | ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Customer Registration                | Customers               | Name, email, phone, address, tax code, account information      | Account creation and service management        | Authorized employees, service providers         | According to applicable retention requirements     |
| Customer Support                     | Customers               | Name, contact details, support requests and related information | Providing customer support                     | Support staff, relevant service providers       | For the time necessary to manage the request       |
| Employee Management                  | Employees               | Identification data, contact details, employment information    | HR and employment management                   | HR, management, payroll providers               | According to legal requirements                    |
| Authentication and Access Management | Customers and employees | Account identifiers, authentication data, access logs           | Account security and access control            | IT and security personnel                       | According to security and operational requirements |
| Marketing Communications             | Customers               | Contact details, marketing preferences                          | Sending promotional communications             | Marketing personnel, relevant service providers | Until consent is withdrawn or no longer necessary  |
| Security Monitoring                  | Customers and employees | IP addresses, logs, device and technical information            | Detecting and investigating security incidents | IT and security personnel                       | According to security and operational requirements |

## ROPA Review

The ROPA shall be reviewed periodically to ensure that processing activities, purposes, categories of data and recipients remain accurate and up to date.

The ROPA shall also be updated when new processing activities are introduced or existing activities are significantly changed.
