# DSAR Process

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this process is to define how Data Subject Access Requests (DSARs) are received, assessed and managed.

## 2. Request Submission

Data subjects may submit a request through the organization's designated communication channels.

Each request shall be recorded in the DSAR register.

## 3. Identity Verification

Before providing personal data, the organization shall verify the identity of the requesting data subject when necessary.

Only the minimum information required for verification shall be collected.

## 4. Request Assessment

The organization shall:

* Identify the data subject and the relevant personal data.
* Determine the scope of the request.
* Identify the systems and departments that may contain relevant data.
* Assess whether any legal restrictions or exceptions apply.

## 5. Response

The organization shall collect the relevant personal data, review the information and prepare the response.

The response shall be reviewed and approved by the responsible function before being sent to the data subject.

Requests and response deadlines shall be monitored through the DSAR register.

## 6. Documentation

The DSAR register should contain, where applicable:

* Request identification number
* Date of receipt
* Data subject
* Request type
* Responsible person
* Actions performed
* Response date
* Final outcome

All relevant documentation shall be stored securely.

## 7. Review

The DSAR process shall be reviewed periodically to ensure that requests are managed consistently and within the applicable GDPR requirements.
