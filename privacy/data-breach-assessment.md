# Data Breach Assessment

## 1. Incident Scenario

A ransomware attack affects a company server containing customer personal data.

The incident causes temporary unavailability of the database and there is a possibility that some personal data may have been accessed or copied by the attacker.

The incident is reported to the IT Department and escalated for assessment.

## 2. Personal Data Involved

The affected system may contain:

* Customer names and contact details
* Addresses
* Tax codes
* Account information
* Financial information
* Authentication and technical information

## 3. Initial Assessment

The incident may involve both the **availability** and **confidentiality** of personal data.

The organization shall assess:

* What personal data was affected
* Which customers may be affected
* Whether unauthorized access or data exfiltration occurred
* The possible impact on affected individuals
* Whether the incident is likely to result in a risk to their rights and freedoms

## 4. Notification Assessment

Based on the results of the investigation, the organization shall determine whether the personal data breach must be notified to the competent supervisory authority.

If notification is required, the organization shall manage it according to the applicable GDPR requirements and document the decision.

If the breach is likely to result in a high risk to affected individuals, the organization shall also assess whether communication to the affected data subjects is required.

## 5. Actions

The organization should:

1. Contain the ransomware incident.
2. Preserve relevant evidence and logs.
3. Identify the affected systems and personal data.
4. Assess the risks to affected individuals.
5. Determine whether notification is required.
6. Document the assessment and decisions.
7. Restore affected systems using verified backups.
8. Perform a post-incident review and implement corrective actions.
