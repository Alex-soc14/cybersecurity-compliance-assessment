# Risk Matrix

## Risk Scoring

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

### Risk Levels

| Score | Level |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Medium |
| 10–16 | High |
| 17–25 | Critical |
