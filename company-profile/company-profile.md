# Company Profile

## Company Overview

**Company name:** FinTech Solutions S.r.l.  
**Industry:** Financial Technology  
**Location:** Italy  
**Employees:** 80  
**Assessment type:** Simulated cybersecurity and compliance assessment

FinTech Solutions S.r.l. is a fictional Italian company operating in the
financial technology sector.

The company provides online financial services through a web-based platform
and processes personal and financial information belonging to its customers.

The organization relies on cloud infrastructure, Microsoft 365, remote
working and several third-party technology providers.

This company is fictional and is used exclusively for educational and
portfolio purposes.

---

## IT Environment

The company environment includes:

- Web application used by customers
- Cloud-hosted servers
- Microsoft 365
- Corporate laptops
- Remote workers
- Internal business applications
- Customer database
- Third-party technology and service providers
- Internet-facing services

---

## Data Processed

The company processes different categories of information, including:

- Customer identification data
- Contact information
- Account information
- Financial information
- Employee information
- Authentication and access data
- Technical and log information

Some of this information constitutes personal data under the GDPR.

---

## Security Context

The company has implemented several basic cybersecurity controls, but its
security and compliance framework is not fully mature.

Some processes are documented, while others are informal or inconsistently
applied.

The organization wants to improve its cybersecurity posture and establish a
more structured approach to:

- Cybersecurity risk management
- Regulatory compliance
- Information security
- Privacy management
- Incident response
- Third-party risk management
- Business continuity

---

## Assessment Objectives

This assessment aims to evaluate the organization's current cybersecurity
and compliance posture against selected requirements and best practices
derived from:

- NIS2
- GDPR
- ISO/IEC 27001

The assessment will identify gaps, evaluate associated risks and propose
appropriate remediation actions.

---

## Assessment Scope

The assessment covers:

- Cybersecurity governance
- Risk management
- Access control
- Incident management
- Business continuity
- Data protection
- Privacy processes
- Third-party security
- Information security documentation
- Security awareness and training
