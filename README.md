# Cybersecurity Compliance Assessment

## Overview

This project is a fictional cybersecurity and compliance assessment created for educational purposes.

The assessment simulates the activities that a junior cybersecurity or GRC professional could perform when evaluating an organization's cybersecurity posture, risks and compliance requirements.

The fictional organization used in this project is **FinTech Solutions S.r.l.**, an Italian fintech company with approximately 80 employees.

## Objectives

The main objectives of the project are to:

* Identify and assess cybersecurity risks.
* Evaluate the organization's security posture.
* Assess selected requirements related to NIS2, GDPR and ISO/IEC 27001.
* Identify security, privacy and compliance gaps.
* Define appropriate remediation actions.
* Create basic cybersecurity and privacy documentation.
* Demonstrate how risks, controls, requirements and remediation activities can be connected.

## Assessment Scope

The assessment covers:

* Cybersecurity governance
* Risk management
* Access control
* Incident management
* Business continuity and disaster recovery
* Vulnerability management
* Security awareness and training
* Data protection and privacy
* Third-party security
* Security policies and documentation

## Methodology

The assessment follows a simplified risk-based approach.

**Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact**

Risk levels are classified as:

| Score | Level    |
| ----- | -------- |
| 1–4   | Low      |
| 5–9   | Medium   |
| 10–16 | High     |
| 17–25 | Critical |

The compliance assessments classify findings as:

* **Compliant**
* **Partially Compliant**
* **Non-Compliant**

The methodology is simplified for educational purposes and does not represent a formal audit or certification methodology.

## Frameworks

The project considers selected requirements and principles from:

* **NIS2 Directive**
* **GDPR**
* **ISO/IEC 27001**

The assessment is not intended to determine the legal applicability of NIS2 to the fictional organization and does not constitute legal advice.

## Key Findings

The assessment identified several areas requiring improvement, including:

* Lack of a formal incident management process.
* Incomplete MFA coverage.
* Weak disaster recovery and backup governance.
* Lack of a formal vulnerability management process.
* Gaps in personal data breach management.
* Informal DSAR management.
* Lack of a formal DPIA process.
* Weak third-party security assessment processes.
* Limited security awareness activities.
* Incomplete information security governance.

## Remediation

The identified gaps are translated into a prioritized remediation plan.

The remediation approach follows the principle:

**Risk → Requirement → Gap → Remediation → Evidence**

This allows each identified weakness to be connected to a concrete improvement action and the evidence that could demonstrate its implementation.

## Disclaimer

This is a fictional educational project created to demonstrate cybersecurity, GRC, risk assessment and compliance concepts.

It does not represent a real company assessment, professional audit, legal opinion, ISO/IEC 27001 certification assessment or NIS2 compliance assessment.

No confidential or real organizational information is used.
